Help fund full-body MRI screening for first responders.

They run into burning buildings, hazardous scenes, wreckage, violence, smoke, and chaos.

They do it for strangers. They do it for our families. They do it for us.

But the danger does not always end when the shift is over. For many first responders, the greatest threat appears years later — quietly, internally, and too often too late.

A full-body MRI can catch it early. But the scan costs $2,000 or more, and insurance almost never covers it — so the men and women most at risk are left to pay out of pocket, or go without.

Your donation changes that. Every $2,000 funds one radiation-free, physician-reviewed scan for an eligible first responder — and helps catch cancer when it is most survivable.
