Our mission

Protecting those who protect us.

Frontline Heroes Foundation was created to help first responders access earlier answers, better information, and practical support around occupational cancer risk.

Donate to the Screening Fund

Why we exist

Our work is focused on one thing: funding full-body MRI screening for eligible first responders through a donor-funded grant program — so cancer can be caught earlier, when it is most treatable.

We believe first responders should not have to wait for symptoms, fight access barriers alone, or put their own health last after years of protecting others.

Because the call doesn’t end at the scene.

How we work

Our commitments

⚖️

Evidence, not hype

Every statistic we publish is cited to its original source. Where the evidence is strongest for firefighters, we say so — and we use careful, exposure-informed language for law enforcement and EMS.

🩹

Physician-guided, always

We are not a medical provider. Grants support access to screening and navigation — the medical decisions stay between responders and licensed clinicians.

🔒

Privacy and dignity

Applications are short and privacy-conscious. We never collect medical records, diagnosis details, Social Security numbers, or insurance IDs through public forms.
